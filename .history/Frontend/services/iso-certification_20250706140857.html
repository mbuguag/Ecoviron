Overview and Legal Requirements

Workplace hygiene audits are guided by the Occupational Safety and Health Act, 2007 (OSHA), and supported by various subsidiary regulations. These laws require employers to maintain a clean, safe, and healthy environment for workers.

  • OSHA Section 6(1): Requires employers to ensure the health, safety, and welfare of employees at work.
  • OSHA Section 13(1): Requires development of workplace policies that include hygiene standards.
  • OSHA Section 74: Requires cleanliness of workplaces including floors, walls, and workstations.
  • Factories (Cleanliness, Ventilation, and Lighting) Rules, Legal Notice No. 44 of 2004: Provides specific requirements on workplace hygiene, lighting, air circulation, and waste handling.
