Overview and Legal Requirements

In Kenya, all workplaces are legally required to comply with both environmental and occupational safety regulations as stipulated under:

  • Occupational Safety and Health Act (OSHA), 2007:
    • Section 6(1): Duty of employer to ensure the safety, health and welfare at work of all employees.
    • Section 11: Obligation to carry out regular risk assessments and implement safety measures.
    • Section 13(1): Requirement for employers to develop and implement a Health and Safety Policy.
    • Section 44: Provision for workplace audits and inspections by a registered safety and health advisor.
  • Environmental Management and Coordination Act (EMCA), 1999:
    • Section 58(1): Requires an Environmental Impact Assessment (EIA) or Environmental Audit (EA) for existing facilities.
    • Legal Notice No. 101 (Environmental Management and Coordination (Environmental Impact Assessment and Audit) Regulations, 2003):
      • Regulation 31: Mandates annual Environmental Audits for ongoing projects or facilities.
      • Regulation 34: Environmental Audit reports must be prepared by registered NEMA experts.

Our Process

At Bionix ESH Solutions, we follow a structured and compliant process guided by the Occupational Safety and Health Act (2007) and the Environmental Management and Coordination Act (1999), along with their subsidiary regulations.

  1. Preliminary Engagement and Site Familiarization:

    We initiate the process with an engagement meeting to understand the scope of operations. A preliminary site visit is conducted to assess the facility’s nature, size, and risks, as required under OSHA Section 11 and EMCA Regulation 31.

  2. Documentation Review

    We review existing statutory documents such as:

      Health and Safety Policy
      Fire Safety Certificates
      Risk Assessment Reports
      Environmental Audit Reports
      Waste Management Plans
      Incident Logs
    This ensures compliance with OSHA Section 13 and EMCA Regulation 34.

  3. Documentation Review

    We review existing statutory documents such as:

      Health and Safety Policy
      Fire Safety Certificates
      Risk Assessment Reports
      Environmental Audit Reports
      Waste Management Plans
      Incident Logs
    This ensures compliance with OSHA Section 13 and EMCA Regulation 34.

  4. Field Inspection and Risk Assessment.

    Our certified safety and environmental auditors conduct a physical walkthrough to assess:

      Safety systems and controls (as per OSHA)
      Environmental impact areas (as per EMCA)
      Waste handling, storage, and disposal
      Emergency preparedness and response
      Use of PPE and safety signage
    This complies with OSHA Sections 6, 11, 44 and EMCA Sections 58 & 68

  5. Employee Interviews and Participation.

    We involve employees to gather insights on workplace conditions and verify compliance with safety standards, in line with OSHA Section 10.

  6. Audit Reporting and Recommendations

    We compile a legally compliant report detailing:

      Areas of non-compliance
      Recommendations for improvement
      Risk levels and priorities
    The report meets the format prescribed under OSHA Regulation 47 and EMCA Audit Guidelines.

  7. Submission to Authorities(If Required)

    Where applicable, we facilitate submission of the Environmental Audit report to NEMA, and prepare audit reports for DOSHS.
