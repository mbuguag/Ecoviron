NEMA and OSHA Audit

Our assessments identify gaps, risks, and areas of non-compliance, and provide actionable recommendations to help organizations meet legal and industry standards.

Overview and Legal Requirements

In Kenya, all workplaces are legally required to comply with both environmental and occupational safety regulations as stipulated under:

  • Occupational Safety and Health Act (OSHA), 2007:
    • Section 6(1): Duty of employer to ensure the safety, health and welfare at work of all employees.
    • Section 11: Obligation to carry out regular risk assessments and implement safety measures.
    • Section 13(1): Requirement for employers to develop and implement a Health and Safety Policy.
    • Section 44: Provision for workplace audits and inspections by a registered safety and health advisor.
  • Environmental Management and Coordination Act (EMCA), 1999:
    • Section 58(1): Requires an Environmental Impact Assessment (EIA) or Environmental Audit (EA) for existing facilities.
    • Legal Notice No. 101:
      • Regulation 31: Mandates annual Environmental Audits for ongoing projects or facilities.
      • Regulation 34: Environmental Audit reports must be prepared by registered NEMA experts.

Our Process

We follow a structured process guided by OSHA (2007) and EMCA (1999).

  1. Preliminary Engagement and Site Familiarization

    We initiate the process with an engagement meeting and conduct a preliminary site visit to assess the facility’s nature, size, and risks, in line with OSHA Section 11 and EMCA Regulation 31.

  2. Documentation Review

    We review the following statutory documents to ensure compliance with OSHA Section 13 and EMCA Regulation 34:

    • Health and Safety Policy
    • Fire Safety Certificates
    • Risk Assessment Reports
    • Environmental Audit Reports
    • Waste Management Plans
    • Incident Logs
  3. Field Inspection and Risk Assessment

    Our auditors conduct a walkthrough to evaluate:

    • Safety systems and controls
    • Environmental impact areas
    • Waste handling, storage, and disposal
    • Emergency preparedness and response
    • Use of PPE and signage
  4. Employee Interviews and Participation

    We involve employees to assess workplace safety culture and OSHA Section 10 compliance.

  5. Audit Reporting and Recommendations

    We compile a report detailing:

    • Non-compliance areas
    • Recommendations for improvement
    • Risk levels and priorities
  6. Submission to Authorities (If Required)

    We help submit reports to NEMA and DOSHS as needed.

  7. Follow-up and Compliance Support

    We assist with implementing recommendations, training, and preparing for inspections.

Why This Audit Is Done

This audit helps to:

  • Ensure legal compliance with Kenyan laws
  • Identify and manage safety/environmental risks
  • Protect employees, the public, and the environment
  • Avoid fines or legal issues from NEMA/DOSHS
  • Promote a safer and more sustainable workplace
