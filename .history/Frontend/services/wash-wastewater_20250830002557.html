WASH and Wastewater Solutions

We design, build, and provide consultancy for sustainable systems including septic systems, anaerobic treatment units, constructed wetlands, and decentralized wastewater treatment systems (DEWATS) tailored to institutional, commercial, and residential needs.

Overview and Legal Requirements

Our WASH and wastewater solutions are guided by Kenyan legal and environmental frameworks including:

  • Environmental Management and Coordination Act (EMCA), 1999: Governs pollution control and waste management.
  • Water Act, 2016: Regulates water supply, wastewater treatment, and discharge standards.
  • Public Health Act (Cap. 242): Requires proper sanitation systems to prevent public health risks.
  • Legal Notice No. 87 (Water Quality Regulations, 2006): Sets standards for effluent discharge into the environment.
  • NEMA Guidelines: Require Environmental Impact Assessments (EIAs) and Audits for sanitation infrastructure projects.

Our Process

  • Feasibility studies and site assessments.
  • Design of sanitation and wastewater treatment systems (ABR, wetlands, septic tanks, DEWATS).
  • Construction and supervision of installations.
  • Environmental Impact Assessments and Audits.
  • System rehabilitation and upgrades.
  • Community engagement, WASH promotion, and training.
  • Compliance support with NEMA, Ministry of Water, and County Regulations.

Why Choose Us?

With years of experience and a qualified team, we ensure that your project gets approved quickly and meets environmental regulations.
