Overview and Legal Requirements

In Kenya, all workplaces are legally required to comply with both environmental and occupational safety regulations as stipulated under:

    Environmental Management and Coordination Act (EMCA), 1999: Governs pollution control and waste managementOccupational Safety and Health Act, 2007 (OSHA)
      Water Act, 2016: Regulates water supply, wastewater treatment, and discharge standards.
    • Public Health Act (Cap. 242): Requires proper sanitation systems to prevent public health risks.
    • Legal Notice No. 87 (Water Quality Regulations, 2006): Sets standards for effluent discharge into the environment.
    • NEMA Guidelines: Require Environmental Impact Assessments (EIAs) and Audits for sanitation infrastructure projects.
