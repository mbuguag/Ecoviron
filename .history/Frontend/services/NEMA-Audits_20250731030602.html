Overview and Legal Requirements

In Kenya, all workplaces are legally required to comply with both environmental and occupational safety regulations as stipulated under:

  • Occupational Safety and Health Act (OSHA), 2007:
    • Section 6(1): Employer's duty to ensure employee safety, health, and welfare.
    • Section 11: Obligation to conduct regular risk assessments.
    • Section 13(1): Health and Safety Policy requirement.
    • Section 44: Workplace audits by registered advisors.
  • Environmental Management and Coordination Act (EMCA), 1999:
    • Section 58(1): EIA or Environmental Audit required for existing facilities.
    • Legal Notice No. 101:
      • Regulation 31: Annual Environmental Audits required.
      • Regulation 34: Reports must be by registered NEMA experts.

Our Process

We follow a structured approach guided by OSHA and EMCA:

  1. Preliminary Engagement & Site Familiarization

    We begin with an engagement meeting and site assessment to understand facility scope and risks.

  2. Documentation Review

    We review required documentation for compliance:

    • Health and Safety Policy
    • Fire Safety Certificates
    • Risk Assessment Reports
    • Environmental Audit Reports
    • Waste Management Plans
    • Incident Logs
  3. Field Inspection & Risk Assessment

    We conduct thorough walkthroughs to evaluate:

    • Safety systems and controls
    • Waste handling and disposal
    • Emergency preparedness
    • PPE usage and signage
  4. Employee Interviews

    We assess employee participation and workplace safety culture.

  5. Audit Report & Recommendations

    We deliver a detailed report with:

    • Non-compliance findings
    • Corrective recommendations
    • Risk levels and priorities
  6. Submission to Authorities

    We assist with submission to NEMA and DOSHS, where applicable.

  7. Follow-up & Support

    We support implementation of recommendations, training, and readiness checks.

  • Avoid fines, shutdowns, or legal action
  • Foster a culture of sustainability and safety
