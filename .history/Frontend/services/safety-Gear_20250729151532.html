Overview and Legal Requirements

Provision and use of PPE and first aid resources in Kenyan workplaces is a legal requirement under the Occupational Safety and Health Act, 2007 (OSHA). Key provisions include:

  • OSHA Section 6(1): Duty of the employer to ensure safety and health of employees at work.
  • OSHA Section 101: Requires employers to provide and maintain adequate and suitable personal protective equipment (PPE).
  • OSHA Section 95: Mandates that workplaces be equipped with appropriate first aid boxes and trained first aiders.
  • Legal Notice No. 160 of 2004: Specifies standards for first aid equipment and training.
