Overview and Legal Requirements

In Kenya, all workplaces are legally required to comply with both environmental and occupational safety regulations as stipulated under:

  • Environmental Management and Coordination Act (EMCA), 1999: Governs pollution control and waste management.
      • Section 6(1): Duty of employer to ensure the safety, health and welfare at work of all employees. Section 11: Obligation to carry out regular risk assessments and implement safety measures. Section 13(1): Requirement for employers to develop and implement a Health and Safety Policy. Section 44: Provision for workplace audits and inspections by a registered safety and health advisor.
  • Environmental Management and Coordination Act, 1999 (EMCA)
      • - Section 58(1): Requires an Environmental Impact Assessment (EIA) or Environmental Audit (EA) for existing facilities. Legal Notice No. 101 (Environmental Management and Coordination (Environmental Impact Assessment and Audit) Regulations, 2003): Regulation 31: Mandates annual Environmental Audits for ongoing projects or facilities. Regulation 34: Environmental Audit reports must be prepared by registered NEMA experts.
