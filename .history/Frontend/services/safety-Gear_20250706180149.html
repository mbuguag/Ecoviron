Overview and Legal Requirements

Provision and use of PPE and first aid resources in Kenyan workplaces is a legal requirement under the Occupational Safety and Health Act, 2007 (OSHA). Key provisions include:

    OSHA Section 6(1): Duty of the employer to ensure safety and health of employees at work.
    OSHA Section 101: Requires employers to provide and maintain adequate and suitable personal protective clothing and equipment.
    OSHA Section 95: Mandates that workplaces must be equipped with appropriate first aid boxes and trained first aiders.
    Factories and Other Places of Work (First Aid) Rules, Legal Notice No. 160 of 2004: Details specific standards for first aid equipment and training.
